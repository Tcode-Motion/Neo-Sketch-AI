Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026 • Version 1.0.1

1 Overview & Core Commitment

NeoSketch AI is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, offline-native creative studio for Android. We believe your artwork, personal photos, and sketches are solely yours.

🔒 Zero Cloud Uploads for Artwork: All image processing, layer rendering, background removal, 3D depth generation, super-resolution upscaling, and pencil sketch conversions are executed 100% on your local device hardware using on-device neural runtimes (ONNX Runtime). Your artwork is never sent to external servers or AI clouds.

2 Information We Collect & How It Is Handled

Because NeoSketch AI operates primarily offline, our data collection is strictly minimal and confined to standard third-party developer SDKs:

  • Local Artwork & Canvas Data: All projects, layer bitmaps, vector strokes, and export snapshots remain exclusively in your device's internal app storage. We have no access to your files.
  • Optional AI Neural Model Downloads: When you set up an AI tool (e.g., Background Removal AI or 3D Depth Generator), the app downloads open-source model weight files directly to your device. No user identifiable data is attached to these download requests.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The free tier of NeoSketch AI utilizes Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic crash logs according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): When upgrading to NeoSketch Pro/Premium, all transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

3 Permissions Requested by NeoSketch AI

To provide full drawing and image processing capabilities, the app requests the following Android system permissions:

  • Photos & Media / Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used solely to allow you to import images from your photo gallery onto the canvas and save exported high-resolution artwork to your device's public Pictures/NeoSketch directory.
  • Network Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively for downloading optional ONNX model files, verifying Google Play subscriptions, and loading AdMob ads.
  • Battery Saver & Performance Optimization (DEVICE_POWER): Used to adjust on-device neural inference and rendering framerates when your device enters Battery Saver mode.

4 Data Retention, Control & Deletion

You retain complete control over all data generated within NeoSketch AI:

  • Deleting Artwork: You can delete any canvas project directly from the in-app Projects screen.
  • Removing Neural Models: You can remove downloaded AI models at any time from the app Settings under "Model Manager" to free up storage space.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing app data via Android system settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local databases, projects, and cached models from your device.

5 Children's Privacy

NeoSketch AI does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6 Third-Party Services & Links

NeoSketch AI integrates the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google Play Services & Billing
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK)
  • Microsoft ONNX Runtime (Local Neural Engine)

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated Effective Date.
